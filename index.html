Common questions

How long does a project take?

A one-page site usually takes about a week. A multi-page site takes two to four weeks, depending on how quickly we agree on content and designs.

How does payment work?

I ask for a deposit to begin, and the balance is due when the work is approved. Larger projects are split into milestones.

Can you update my site later?

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of free fixes, and I offer monthly care plans after that.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the code, design files and content are yours.
